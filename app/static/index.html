About this prototype

        
Decision support for a fictional airline. Every airport, event and number is simulated by
          the organizers — never present it as real-world data.

      

    

    

      
What it does

      
Four parts in one app: a three-comparison anomaly detector; a fact sheet
        whose numbers come from the workbook, an offline writer and a checker;
        an exam runner; and an append-only feedback memory. The writer never
        computes a number, and decisions stay with qualified people.

Limitations

      
    
        
  • The organizer data is monthly and airport-level — it cannot identify individual flights or crews.

  • Weather is background evidence; it is not proof of causation.
    • 
        
  • The demo writer is a deterministic template; the optional live-LLM writer is not enabled in this run.

  • When the checker is not available, the UI shows "not checked" rather than faking a pass.
