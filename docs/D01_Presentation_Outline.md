## Framing: we are the airline's operations assistant
The brief casts us as consultants for **one fictional Chinese domestic airline**. Say this on the title slide and keep
every line in the airline's voice:
- **Who uses it:** the airline's operations-control duty staff, not the airport. The assistant answers "which of *our*
  airports needs attention first, and what do *we* check, escalate and tell?"
- **Why the airline view matters:** each airport's priority combines its anomaly with its **importance to our airline**
  (sheet 02, 航空公司业务重要程度 高/中/低). The network view and schematic show this ranking.
- **Airline vs airport roles:** the three drafts follow the real split.
  - f13 goes to our own operations staff.
  - f14 goes to the **airport coordination team**, a partner we ask to confirm capacity, not order.
  - f15 is a passenger draft that a person reviews.
- **Decisions stay with the airline's qualified people:** cancellation, release, dispatch, maintenance and
  compensation (f12, RULE007).

Suggested title line: "An operations assistant for our airline's disruption desk — it can't make numbers up."
