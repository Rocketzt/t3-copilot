# Submission draft

## Title

Our Airline's Disruption Desk

## Short description

We help our airline's operations staff rank airport risks, verify source-backed facts, and prepare distinct messages for operations, airport coordination, and passengers. All data and results are simulated; qualified people retain operational decisions.

## Project description (150 words)

Our airline needs to decide which airport deserves attention first when weather, monthly performance and operational reports point in conflicting directions. We built a simulated disruption desk that ranks eight fictional airports using detector signals and each airport's importance to our business. For a selected event, it shows source-backed facts, missing data, uncertainty and a risk assessment without presenting monthly patterns as proof of a flight delay. It drafts notes for our operations staff, the airport coordination team and passengers. Our staff use those drafts to check aircraft, crew, gate and weather information before any public message. The checker blocks unsupported numbers, private details and claims that exceed the evidence. Human feedback is retained and recalled so reviews can reflect prior decisions while verifying current conditions. Cancellation, release, dispatch, maintenance and compensation remain with qualified airline professionals. This offline prototype uses fictional organizer data and labels every result as simulated.
